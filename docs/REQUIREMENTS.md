# Requirements

## Business Goals
- Track loan portfolio health with focus on Non-Performing Loans (NPL)
- Alert business performance teams when accounts move into high-risk zones
- Provide fast reporting for branch and province-level decisions

## Functional Scope
- Dashboard KPIs for total loans, NPL count, NPL ratio, and total outstanding
- Loan table with risk-level badges and Days Past Due (DPD)
- Alert list for high-risk and critical accounts
- Analytics charts for trend and exposure distribution
- CSV export for reporting
- Mock-data mode now, Google Sheets integration ready for later

## Non-Functional
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Clear separation of frontend, backend, and services
- Configurable environment variables for API and future sheet credentials
